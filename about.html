About LBEF/APU Hackathon 2026

Fostering student innovation and practical technology solutions.

Academic Collaboration

The LBEF/APU Hackathon 2026 marks a premium academic initiative bridging the Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF) in Kathmandu, Nepal, with the Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

This undergraduate level hackathon is specifically structured as part of the FDD (Fundamentals of Web Design and Development) module. It provides students with a playground to apply client-side scripting, structured layout styling, and semantic content mapping to solve realistic digital problems.

Through team collaboration, mentoring workshops, and expert evaluations, students gain comprehensive project lifecycle exposure, enhancing their future career prospects in global technology industries.

Objectives & Purpose

Our core goals are structured around skill enhancement, innovation, and practical learning.

💡

Encourage Innovation

Motivate students to devise original solutions to current local and international digital challenges.

🧩

Problem-Solving

Develop critical thinking by selecting and breaking down real-world challenge parameters.

👥

Promote Teamwork

Provide experience in Git coordination, code merging, split tasks, and aligned development timelines.

⚙️

Improve Tech Skills

Implement core frontend technologies including semantic HTML5 elements, clean CSS3 layouts, and vanilla JS.

📈

Practical Solutions

Focus on creating working interactive prototypes rather than static designs or slide deck pitches.

🎤

Presentation Skills

Enhance communication skills by defending coding strategies and project design choices before judges.

Hackathon Format

Duration: 24 Hours Sprint

Target Audience: Undergraduate students enrolled in computing, information technology, engineering, and digital business modules at LBEF and APU.

Mode: Blended format with physical presentation hubs in Kathmandu, Nepal, and digital linkage for review panels from APU Malaysia.

Evaluation: Projects will be presented to an academic and industrial panel based on visual layout, coding cleanliness, responsiveness, and problem solving viability.
