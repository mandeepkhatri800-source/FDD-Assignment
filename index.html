About the Hackathon

The LBEF/APU Hackathon 2026 is a student-driven technology platform combining the academic excellence of Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF), Nepal, and the technological prestige of Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU), Malaysia.

This event challenges undergraduate students to build working prototypes addressing real-world problems in key tracks like Web Development, AI, Cybersecurity, Cloud, and Digital Innovation.

Event Highlights

Discover the full scope of activities during the event.

  • Competitions: Multi-track 24-hour sprint where you compete against fellow undergraduate teams.
  • Workshops: Expert-led sessions covering core APIs, web technologies, and presentation pitches.
  • Team Building: Icebreaking activities to collaborate with mentors, peers, and external sponsors.
  • Final Presentation: Formal science-fair style demonstration and final deck pitching.
