Common Questions

Click on any question header below to expand and view the detailed response.

What is the LBEF/APU Hackathon? +

It is a student-focused technology competition co-designed by Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF) Nepal and Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) Malaysia. Students collaborate to create digital prototypes solving specific real-world problems.

Who can participate? +

Any active undergraduate student enrolled in computer science, information technology, engineering, or related business tracks at LBEF or APU campuses.

How many members can be in a team? +

Teams must consist of at least 2 members and a maximum of 4 members. Single entries are not allowed to foster coordination experiences.

How do I register? +

Navigate to the Registration Page on this website, fill in your team details, and check the agreement terms.

Can I participate individually? +

No, you cannot. All submissions must represent collective efforts of at least 2 student developers to match academic evaluation requirements.

What challenge categories are available? +

We host 5 major tracks: Web Development, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, and Digital Innovation.

Do I need advanced programming skills? +

No. The competition welcomes all skill levels. Introductory courses materials (HTML, CSS, basic JS) are sufficient to build interactive prototypes for presentation.

Is the registration form real? +

No, this website acts as a frontend academic assessment mockup. No details are transmitted or stored in database servers.

When is the final presentation? +

Presentations are scheduled at 04:00 PM in the Main Auditorium, following the prototype submission deadline at 03:00 PM. Review the timeline on our Schedule Page.
