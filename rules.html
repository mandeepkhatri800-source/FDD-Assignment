Rules & Eligibility

Ensure your team meets the academic requirements and follows competition regulations.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Only undergraduate students currently enrolled at LBEF or APU are eligible to participate.
  • Students must register in teams of 2 to 4 members. Individual entries are not permitted.
  • Each team must select exactly one challenge category track (Web Dev, AI, Cyber, Cloud, or Digital Innovation).
  • Participants must comply with university academic code of conduct throughout the event.

General Competition Rules

  • Respect all competing teams, lecturers, mentors, and organizing staff members.
  • All project code and documentation must be submitted strictly within the specified deadline.
  • Your software prototype must be explained and demonstrated live by the team members during judging.
  • Academic integrity must be maintained. Copying, stealing, or utilizing pre-built templates without disclosure is prohibited.
  • Prohibited materials: Pre-compiled proprietary frameworks or backend architectures not accessible client-side are disallowed.
  • Teams must write their own code. Utilizing third-party modules is permitted only for mock integration (e.g. sample static API shapes).

Sample Judging Criteria

Evaluation is based on a total score of 100%, distributed as follows:

25%

Innovation

Originality, novelty of idea, and creative approach to solving the track problem.

20%

Relevance

How well the prototype maps to the designated challenge description and goals.

25%

Implementation

Cleanliness of HTML/CSS coding structures and functionality of JavaScript validation.

15%

User Experience

Visual layout appeal, mobile responsiveness, and smooth client transitions.

15%

Presentation

Clarity of project pitch, demonstration of code logic, and handling panel Q&A.
