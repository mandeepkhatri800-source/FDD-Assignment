Event Schedule

Plan your day and follow the key milestones of the hackathon.

Program Timeline

Please note that timestamps represent Local Nepalese Time (NPT). Ensure your team arrives on time for presentations.

Time Activity Description Location
08:30 AM — 09:00 AM Team Registration & Check-in Verify registrations, collect hacking kits, and set up team workspaces. Main Auditorium Lobby
09:00 AM — 09:30 AM Opening Ceremony Welcome address by LBEF Academic Lead and keynotes from APU Malaysia panel. Main Auditorium
09:30 AM — 10:00 AM Challenge Briefing Detailed explanation of challenge parameters, resources, rules, and rubric guidelines. Main Auditorium
10:00 AM — 01:00 PM Hackathon Hacking Sprint — Phase 1 Ideation, repository initialization, wireframing, and backend scaffolding. Hacking Arena (Lab A & B)
01:00 PM — 02:00 PM Technical Mentoring Workshop Hands-on mentoring session for frontend deployment, responsive styling, and web validation. Seminar Hall 1
02:00 PM — 03:00 PM Hackathon Hacking Sprint — Phase 2 Code optimization, script validation, and final styling. Hacking Arena (Lab A & B)
03:00 PM — 04:00 PM Prototype Submission & Coffee Submit GitHub repositories or code ZIPs. Set up live demo presentations. Submission Portal / Lobby
04:00 PM — 05:30 PM Team Presentations & Judging Present solutions to judges (3 minutes presentation + 2 minutes Q&A per team). Main Auditorium
05:30 PM — 06:00 PM Results & Closing Ceremony Award distribution, feedback remarks, photo sessions, and event closing. Main Auditorium
